More animals than lines on the form

Print an animal list sheet with the same columns as the submission form, Tube # and Animal #/ID, 40 lines a sheet. Write on a blank one, or paste your list from your herd program or spreadsheet and print the sheets with the tube numbers filled in.

New: please call the lab at (715) 653-2201 before your first box with a list sheet, so we know it is coming.

How it works

  1. Fill in the top of the submission form and sign it, like always.
  2. Make your list sheet: print a blank one and write it in, or paste your list below and print it.
  3. Number each tube with the tube number on the sheet, plus the animal ID.
  4. Write the total number of tubes on the form.
  5. Staple the list sheets behind the form and put them in the box.

A blank sheet to write on

Print as many as you need. Fill in the top, then write the tube number and the animal ID on each line, in the order you pack the tubes.

Use your browser's Print to print it. Only the sheet prints, on its own page.

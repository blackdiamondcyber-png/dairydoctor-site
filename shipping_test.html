Packaging and Shipping Your Samples

Follow these steps to get your blood or ear notch samples to our lab safely and quickly.

Steps

How to Pack Your Shipment

  1. Label all blood tubes and ear notch vials clearly with the animal ID, making certain caps fit securely.
  2. Place blood tubes and ear notch vials in air-tight, heavy-duty zip-lock bag(s).
  3. Place the bag of samples into the shipping box and surround it with ample packing material to prevent breakage during shipping.
  4. Place the completed submission form(s) and payment into a separate zip-lock bag and include it in the shipment.
  5. Place the completed submission form and payment into the shipping box.
  6. Use proper shipping labels (we can provide them) and write the following on the outside of the box: "BOVINE BLOOD and/or TISSUE SAMPLES."
  7. Ship samples via Priority Mail, FedEx or UPS so they reach our laboratory no later than Wednesday.

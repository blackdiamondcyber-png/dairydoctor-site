Packaging and shipping your samples

Follow these steps to get your blood or ear notch samples to our lab safely. Samples that reach the lab by Wednesday are reported Thursday.

On this page: Packing Planner Address Which form Forms Supplies

How to pack your shipment

  1. Label all blood tubes and ear notch vials clearly with the animal ID, making certain caps fit securely.
  2. Wrap the tubes and vials in absorbent material, then place them in air-tight, heavy-duty zip-lock bag(s).
  3. Place the bag of samples into the shipping box and surround it with ample packing material to prevent breakage during shipping. Use a sturdy cardboard box, never a plain or padded envelope.
  4. Place the completed submission form and payment in their own zip-lock bag inside the box. Not sure which form? See Which form do I need? below. First box? Please call our office before your first submission.
  5. Ear notches and BVD blood samples only (cattle and bison): refrigerate them until you pack, then add frozen ice packs. Pregnancy blood tubes do not need ice. If a cold shipment cannot reach the lab within 48 hours, call first for instructions.
  6. Use proper shipping labels (we can provide them). Write "BOVINE BLOOD and/or TISSUE SAMPLES" and "Exempt Animal Specimen" on the outside of the box. For goats or sheep, write "CAPRINE BLOOD SAMPLES" or "OVINE BLOOD SAMPLES" in place of the first line.
  7. Ship samples via FedEx, UPS or USPS Priority Mail so they reach our laboratory no later than Wednesday. Ear notches and BVD blood must also arrive within 48 hours of collection, whichever comes first.

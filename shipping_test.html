Packing and shipping your samples

Follow these steps to get your blood or ear notch samples to the lab safely. Samples that reach the lab by Wednesday are reported Thursday.

On this page: Packing Planner Address Forms Barn card Supplies

How to pack your shipment

  1. Number each blood tube and ear notch vial in the order of the form, and write the animal ID on it, making certain caps fit securely.
  2. Wrap the tubes and vials in absorbent material, then place them in air-tight, heavy-duty zip-lock bag(s).
  3. Place the bag of samples into the shipping box and surround it with ample packing material to prevent breakage during shipping. Use a sturdy cardboard box, never a plain or padded envelope.
  4. Place the completed submission form and payment in their own zip-lock bag inside the box. Not sure which form? See Submission forms below.
  5. Ear notches and BVD blood samples only (cattle and bison): refrigerate them until you pack, then add frozen ice packs. Pregnancy blood tubes do not need ice. If a cold shipment cannot reach the lab within 48 hours, call first for instructions.
  6. Use proper shipping labels (we can provide them). Write "BOVINE BLOOD and/or TISSUE SAMPLES" and "Exempt Animal Specimen" on the outside of the box. For goats or sheep, write "CAPRINE BLOOD SAMPLES" or "OVINE BLOOD SAMPLES" in place of the first line.
  7. Ship by FedEx, UPS or USPS Priority Mail so the box reaches the lab no later than Wednesday. Cold samples (ear notches and BVD blood) must also arrive within 48 hours of collection.

Ship-by planner

Pick the day the box goes out and how it travels. The planner shows the day it reaches the lab and the Thursday report it makes.

Days from shipping to arrival at the lab. Overnight means a next-day carrier.

Ear notches and BVD blood ship cold. Pregnancy tubes do not need ice.

Boxes that reach the lab Monday to Wednesday are in that Thursday's report. With Priority Mail, usually 2 days, ship by Monday. With overnight shipping, ship by Tuesday. A box shipped later is in the next week's report.

The Postal Service does not deliver on federal holidays, and next-day carriers do not deliver on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving or Christmas, or on the Friday or Monday when one of those is observed. A box that would arrive on a weekend or one of those days counts from the next lab day. If the report Thursday is a holiday, call (715) 653-2201 for that week's report day. Ear notches and BVD blood ship cold and must reach the lab within 48 hours of collection, so they should not wait over a weekend or a holiday.

When to draw
Cows and bison from 28 days after breeding and at least 90 days since calving. Virgin heifers from 25 days. Goats and sheep from 30 days.
The draw
At least 2cc of whole blood in a red-top tube, a new needle for each animal. Cattle and bison from the tail vein, goats and sheep from the jugular vein. Number each tube in the order of the form, and write the animal ID on it.
Ice
Pregnancy tubes need no ice. Ear notches and BVD blood ship with frozen ice packs and must reach the lab within 48 hours of collection.
The box
A sturdy box, never an envelope. Tubes in a sealed zip-lock bag with padding. Form and check (payable to ADDC) in their own bag. Write "BOVINE BLOOD and/or TISSUE SAMPLES" (or CAPRINE or OVINE BLOOD SAMPLES) and "Exempt Animal Specimen" on the outside.
The week
Tests run Monday to Thursday. Samples that reach the lab by Wednesday are reported Thursday.

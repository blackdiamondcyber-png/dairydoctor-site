How it works

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Collect samples

    Draw a blood sample from the tail vein with a new needle for each animal. See the tail bleeding how-to guide.

  2. Step 2

    Fill out the right form

    Download the form that matches your samples: Cattle/Bison Pregnancy Form, Goat/Sheep Pregnancy Form, or BVD PI Test Form.

  3. Step 3

    Ship by midweek

    Pack your samples and form well, and ship so they reach our lab no later than Wednesday. Full shipping instructions here.

  4. Step 4

    Results Thursday

    Samples that reach the lab by Wednesday are reported Thursday by email or fax, in time to put open animals on next week's breeding list. Need results sooner? Call ahead and we will arrange it.

Plan a draw

When can I test?

Cows and bison can be sampled 28 days after breeding, virgin heifers at 25, goats and sheep at 30. Samples that reach the lab by Wednesday are reported that Thursday.

Who the lab works with

The lab works by mail, so it suits any herd that can draw its own samples.

  • Beef cow-calf herds

    Check at weaning or a month after the bull comes out, and cull on the answer instead of wintering an open cow.

  • Dairies checking between vet visits

    Your own crew draws on your schedule. The open list is back before the next protocol day.

  • Custom heifer growers

    Off-site heifers get checked without a vet visit to the lot. The grower's crew draws on a day they are already in the chute.

  • Goat and sheep dairies

    Goats and sheep run on the same blood test as cattle, drawn from the jugular vein.

  • Bison ranches

    The assay is validated in bison, and nobody wants to palpate one. Draw at the handling system, mail the tubes.

  • Large dairies: records and programs

    Records review, preventive programs, biosecurity and disease monitoring with Dr. Pearson, by phone and email.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Collect samples

    Draw a blood sample from the tail vein (goats and sheep: the jugular vein) with a new needle for each animal. See the tail bleeding how-to guide.

  2. Step 2

    Fill out the right form

    Use the form that matches your samples: cattle and bison, goats and sheep, or BVD PI. All three are just below these steps.

  3. Step 3

    Ship by midweek

    Pack your samples and form well, and ship so they reach the lab no later than Wednesday. Full shipping instructions here.

  4. Step 4

    Results Thursday

    Samples that reach the lab by Wednesday are reported Thursday by email, fax or phone, whichever you mark on the form. That is in time to put open animals on next week's breeding list.

When can I test?

Cows and bison from 28 days after breeding, virgin heifers from 25, goats and sheep from 30. For goats and sheep, if the buck or ram ran with them, enter the day he came out.

For a cow that has calved. Cows should be at least 90 days since calving.

Days from shipping to arrival at the lab. Overnight means a next-day carrier.

Cows and bison can be sampled 28 days after breeding, virgin heifers at 25, goats and sheep at 30. If that date is today or past, she can be sampled now.

Boxes that reach the lab Monday to Wednesday are in that Thursday's report. With Priority Mail, usually 2 days, ship by Monday. With overnight shipping, ship by Tuesday. A box shipped later is in the next week's report.

Priority Mail usually takes 2 days but is not guaranteed. Shipping a day earlier, or overnight, leaves a margin. The Postal Service does not deliver on federal holidays, and next-day carriers do not deliver on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving or Christmas, or on the Friday or Monday when one of those is observed. A box that would arrive on a weekend or one of those days counts from the next lab day. If the report Thursday is a holiday, call (715) 653-2201 for that week's report day.

Our lab is closed to the public

For the biosecurity of every herd we test, we do not allow visitors into the laboratory. Samples come to us by mail, and Dr. Pearson is available by phone or email during staffed hours. Keeping outside traffic out of the lab is part of how we protect the integrity of your results.

Tests run Monday to Thursday. Samples that reach the lab by Wednesday are reported Thursday.

Results come back by email, fax or phone, whichever you mark on the form.

Our lab is closed to the public

For the biosecurity of every herd we test, we do not allow visitors into the laboratory. Samples come to us by mail, and Dr. Pearson is available by phone or email during staffed hours.

Which form do I need?

Download the right submission form before you collect samples. Pick your animals and the test.

Disease monitoring covers Johne's, Leukosis, Mycoplasma and other tests.

Three forms cover everything:

  • Cattle and bison pregnancy: Cattle-Bison Pregnancy Test Submission Form (PDF)
  • Goat and sheep pregnancy: Small Ruminant Pregnancy Test Submission Form (PDF)
  • BVD PI, cattle and bison only: BVD PI Test Submission Form (PDF)

For Johne's, Leukosis, Mycoplasma and other monitoring, call the lab first at (715) 653-2201.

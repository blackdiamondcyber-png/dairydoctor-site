Herd health consulting

Dr. Glenn Pearson, DVM, will analyze current health and diagnostic trends within your herd and help you formalize the best management response. It starts with a phone call.

On this page: What he looks at What to send How it works How it starts Already testing

What he looks at

A records review looks for things like these in your own records:

  • Cows treated again and again for the same problem
  • Culls with no written reason
  • Purchased animals that were never tested
  • Pregnancy rechecks that bunch up

Proper medical decision-making can have a major impact on cow health and ultimately farm profitability. As your consultant, Dr. Pearson provides:

  • Herd health and treatment records review and analysis
  • Customized preventive programs and treatment plans for your herd
  • Dairy Comp 305 records consulting
  • Biosecurity program design
  • Disease monitoring programs: BVD, Johne's, Leukosis and Mycoplasma
  • Personalized training in on-farm procedures like tail bleeding and ear notching

Johne's, Leukosis and Mycoplasma programs are set up with Dr. Pearson. Call (715) 653-2201 so the right samples go in the box.

For a large herd, a program can take in heifer checks, BVD PI screening of every calf, disease monitoring, and Dairy Comp 305 records review with the veterinarian. Read more on testing heifers and the whole-herd BVD program.

How it works

Dr. Pearson offers online consulting. He works from your herd health and treatment records and your lab results, and goes over what he finds with you by phone and email.

The aim is that you know you are treating your cows properly, every time.

How it starts

  1. Call Dr. Pearson at (715) 653-2201 and tell him about the herd. The lab is staffed Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Central. Rather write? Email Dr. Pearson with a few lines about the herd.
  2. Send the records you already keep. What he asks you to send lists what to gather.
  3. Agree on what you need: a one-time review or a standing program.

About Dr. Glenn Pearson, DVM

A 1985 graduate of the University of Illinois College of Veterinary Medicine, Glenn served for 15 years in traditional dairy practice in Cumberland, Wisconsin, prior to opening Dairy Pharm Services in 1999.

Married in 1988, Glenn and his wife Elaine have since been blessed with three sons. When he's not at Advanced Dairy Diagnostic & Consulting, Glenn is usually involved in his family's church, school and sports activities.

About Advanced Dairy Diagnostic & Consulting, LLC

As the first BioPRYN® affiliate lab east of the Rockies, Advanced Dairy Diagnostic & Consulting began operations in 1999 under the original name of Dairy Pharm Services. Since their affiliation in 2005, ADDC continues to support their clientele with more than 20 years of priceless, proven experience using BioPRYN.

As a client of ADDC, you automatically receive the expert troubleshooting and support of Dr. Glenn Pearson, DVM. With more than 40 years of dairy and beef production medicine experience, Dr. Glenn is only an email or phone call away from helping you with timely intervention and up-to-date corrective advice.

ADDC has clients in all 48 contiguous states, benefiting from this unique combination of reproductive knowledge and pioneering technologies such as BioPRYN. In addition to BioPRYN, ADDC also offers BVD, Johnes, Leukosis, Mycoplasma and other disease monitoring programs.
